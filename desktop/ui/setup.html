How it works

Beam sits by the clock, out of the way, until you need it.

  1. 1
    Press or click the Beam icon by the clock. If it's hidden, click ^ there and drag Beam onto the taskbar.
  2. 2
    Drop files on it, or press Ctrl+VPick who gets it from the buttons under the drop zone: your phone, or a friend's laptop or phone.
  3. 3
    Or right-click any file → Send toOn Windows 11 that's under Show more options.
  4. 4
    Things sent to you pop upwith a notification. Drag them out of Beam, or click to open or copy. Text is copied for you.
